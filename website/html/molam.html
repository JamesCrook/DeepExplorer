Molam — Molecules & Metabolism

Molam is an interactive molecular viewer that runs entirely in your browser using WebGL. It lets you load, navigate, and inspect protein structures and metabolic networks with a real-time 3D interface.

What Molam can do

  • Browse large structure libraries — scroll through lists of 200,000+ proteins; only visible entries are rendered so performance stays smooth regardless of library size.
  • 3D molecular visualisation — rotate, zoom, and examine structures in real time using hardware-accelerated WebGL rendering.
  • Flexible layouts — switch between linear list, radial, and other spatial arrangements to reveal relationships between molecules.
  • Metabolic pathways — trace compounds through metabolic networks and explore how molecules connect across pathways.

Requirements

Molam requires JavaScript and a browser with WebGL support. JavaScript is currently disabled in your browser. Enable it in your browser settings and reload this page to launch Molam.
