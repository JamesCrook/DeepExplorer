deep: (Today at 09:00) **Function**

> [!card] Transcriptional regulator 90/60
> Acts as a tetramer binding a specific response element, activating genes
> that arrest the cell cycle, repair DNA, or trigger apoptosis. Held at low
> abundance by continuous degradation until stress signals interrupt it —
> so the switch is stabilisation, not synthesis.

> [!card] Why the mutation spectrum is unusual 82/45
> Most tumour suppressors are lost by truncation. This one is dominated by
> missense substitutions in the DNA-binding domain, which produce a full-length
> protein that still tetramerises. A mutant subunit can poison a wild-type
> tetramer, which is why a single altered allele can matter.
